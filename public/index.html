    How to use

    Play runs the full sequence: assembled overview → exploded parts kit → every assembly step → completed product. The highlighted part, the BOM row and the step card always refer to the same step.

    SpacePlay / pause
    ← →Previous / next step
    EToggle exploded view
    RRestart
    FFrame model
    GGhost parts not yet installed
    IIsolate selection
    EscClear selection

    Click a part in 3D or a row in the BOM to select it. Drag to orbit, right-drag / two-finger drag to pan, scroll / pinch to zoom.
